About

I am an applied statistician and data scientist with expertise in computational statistical machine learning, deep learning, and their application to clinical and engineering outcomes. My research spans theoretical development of machine learning methods and their direct translation to cardiovascular and thoracic surgery practice.

At Cleveland Clinic I lead data science for CORR, supporting registry-based outcomes research across the Heart, Vascular & Thoracic Institute. I hold a PhD in Statistics from Case Western Reserve University (2011) and earlier graduate degrees in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering.
